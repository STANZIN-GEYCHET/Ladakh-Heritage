Top Tourist Attractions

Explore the breathtaking beauty of Ladakh's mountains, lakes, monasteries and valleys.

Pangong Lake

A stunning high-altitude lake famous for its crystal-clear blue waters and changing colours.

Nubra Valley

Known for sand dunes, double-humped Bactrian camels and spectacular Himalayan scenery.

Thiksey Monastery

A magnificent Buddhist monastery offering panoramic views and spiritual heritage.

Leh Palace

A historic royal palace built in the 17th century overlooking the city of Leh.

Khardung La

One of the world's highest motorable mountain passes with breathtaking landscapes.

Magnetic Hill

A fascinating location where vehicles appear to roll uphill because of an optical illusion.

Tsokar lake

The Tso Kar basin in Ladakh is a high-altitude wetland complex and one of India's most crucial breeding grounds for the vulnerable and majestic black-necked crane.
