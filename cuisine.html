Traditional Cuisine of Ladakh

Discover the authentic flavours of the Himalayas through Ladakh's traditional food.

🍜 Thukpa

A warm noodle soup with vegetables or meat, perfect for Ladakh's cold climate.

🥟 Momos

Steamed dumplings filled with vegetables or meat, one of Ladakh's most popular dishes.

🥘 Skyu

A traditional pasta dish cooked with fresh vegetables and local spices.

🍵 Butter Tea

A salty tea prepared with butter and yak milk, providing warmth and energy.

🥖 Timok

Soft steamed bread served with curries and vegetable dishes.

🧀 Chhurpi

A traditional cheese made from yak or cow milk, enjoyed as a snack or ingredient.
