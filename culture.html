Culture of Ladakh

Experience the traditions, lifestyle, art, music and spirituality of the Land of High Passes.

👘 Traditional Dress

Men and women wear the traditional Goncha, made from thick wool to protect against Ladakh's cold climate. Colourful hats and handcrafted jewellery complete the attire.

🗣️ Languages

Ladakhi is the primary language. Hindi, English and Tibetan are also spoken across different regions.

💃 Music & Dance

Traditional dances such as Cham Dance are performed during monastery festivals. Folk songs celebrate nature, family and local legends.

🙏 Religion

Buddhism plays a major role in Ladakhi culture. Monasteries such as Hemis, Thiksey and Diskit preserve centuries-old traditions.

🍵 Daily Life

Hospitality is an important value in Ladakh. Butter tea, local cuisine and community celebrations reflect the warm spirit of the people.

🎨 Art & Handicrafts

Ladakh is known for Thangka paintings, wood carving, handmade carpets, silver jewellery and traditional weaving.
