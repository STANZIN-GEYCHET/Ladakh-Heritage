Festivals of Ladakh

Celebrate the vibrant traditions, dances, music and spirituality of Ladakh.

🎭 Hemis Festival

The Hemis Festival is Ladakh's most famous festival, celebrated at Hemis Monastery. It features colourful Cham dances, traditional music and prayers honouring Guru Padmasambhava.

🎊 Losar Festival

Losar marks the Ladakhi New Year. Families decorate their homes, prepare traditional food, perform rituals and pray for peace, happiness and prosperity.

🪔 Dosmoche Festival

Celebrated mainly in Leh and Likir monasteries, Dosmoche is known as the Festival of the Scapegoat. Monks perform sacred rituals to remove negativity and bring good fortune.

🏇 Ladakh Festival

This cultural festival showcases folk dances, archery competitions, polo matches, handicrafts, music and local cuisine, attracting visitors from around the world.

🙏 Sindhu Darshan

Held on the banks of the Indus River, Sindhu Darshan celebrates India's cultural diversity through prayers, music and cultural performances.

🎨 Yuru Kabgyat

Celebrated at Lamayuru Monastery, this festival is famous for colourful masked dances, Buddhist rituals and spiritual ceremonies.
