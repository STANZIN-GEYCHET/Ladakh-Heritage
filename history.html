History of Ladakh

Explore the fascinating journey of Ladakh through centuries of trade, culture, and heritage.

Ancient Silk Route

Ladakh was an important stop on the ancient Silk Route, connecting India with Tibet, China and Central Asia. Merchants travelled through Ladakh carrying silk, spices, wool, tea and precious stones.

The Namgyal Dynasty

During the 16th century, the Namgyal kings united Ladakh and built famous monuments like Leh Palace while encouraging Buddhist culture and architecture.

Modern Ladakh

Today, Ladakh is a Union Territory of India known for its breathtaking landscapes, monasteries, unique traditions and rich cultural heritage.
